Project Overview

GreenLink is an ESG risk assessment and supply chain intelligence platform that combines Sentinel-2 satellite remote sensing with AI-powered public sentiment analysis to provide precise, real-time risk scoring separated into Environmental (E) and Social (S) dimensions.

Core Innovation: Traditional ESG ratings (MSCI, Sustainalytics) give a single vague score. GreenLink separates Environmental Risk (verified by satellite) from Social Risk (detected via sentiment), enabling much more accurate risk management and compliance decisions.

Key Features

🛰️ Satellite Monitoring (E)

Uses Sentinel-2 multispectral imagery to detect deforestation, land-use change, and vegetation health at 10m resolution. Provides objective, auditable evidence for EUDR compliance.

📢 AI Sentiment Analysis (S)

Monitors global news, regulatory announcements, and public reports to identify labor issues, forced labor allegations, and social controversies in real time.

🔗 Supply Chain Penetration

Visualizes risk transmission from upstream suppliers → midstream processors → downstream markets, with clear regulatory impact analysis (EUDR, CBP, etc.).

📄 Automated PDF Reports

One-click generation of professional ESG compliance reports with bilingual (Chinese/English) support, suitable for investors, regulators, and internal use.

📱 B2C Traceability

Consumer-facing product verification page with QR code, carbon footprint, origin verification, and blockchain evidence hash.

💰 Green Finance Module

Simulates ESG-linked loan pricing and dynamic credit adjustment based on real-time supplier ESG scores.
